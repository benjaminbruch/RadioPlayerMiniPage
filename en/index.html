Features

Everything a radio player needs — nothing else.

No cluttered interface, no unnecessary extras. Just the things that actually matter when listening to the radio.

Worldwide station search

Thousands of stations like Jazz FM or Deutschlandfunk — just search and add with one click. Powered by the open radio-browser.info database.

Custom design

Surface, accent color, and window size all adjustable — exactly to your taste.

Favorites & your own list

Add stations, rename them, and drag & drop them into exactly the order you want.

AirPlay

Send the stream to speakers or Apple TV with one click.

iCloud sync

Your list and favorites available on all your Macs — off by default, your choice.

Accessibility

Full VoiceOver support, and reduced motion / reduced transparency are respected.

6 languages

German, English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, and Italian — fully translated.

Export & import

Save your station list to a file, or restore it on another Mac.

Discover

No specific station in mind? No problem.

Filter by country or genre, select several stations by checkbox, and add them all at once.

Privacy

Your data stays your data.

Radio Player Mini doesn't track your location and shows no ads. There's no user account and no marketing analytics tools — just anonymous crash reports so bugs can get fixed quickly.

  • ✓ No ads, no user tracking
  • ✓ No account required
  • ✓ iCloud sync is optional and off by default
  • ✓ All data can be deleted completely with one click
